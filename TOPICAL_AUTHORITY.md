# Topical Authority — phenolic.id

## Role and boundary

`phenolic.id` should become an Indonesian material-selection and project-delivery reference for phenolic panels: what the names mean, how product construction changes performance, which evidence supports a claimed property, how designers detail and fabricators machine panels, and how owners procure, accept, clean, inspect, repair, and replace them. The site may cover compact phenolic, phenolic plywood, decorative laminate, exterior-grade, fire-performance, and antibacterial/hygiene applications because these are established commercial routes.

Neutral guides must not turn marketing labels into guaranteed properties. Water, chemical, impact, UV, fire, smoke, emissions, hygiene, structural, slip, and durability claims are product-and-system specific. Publication requires the current product data sheet, test report/certificate with scope and edition, installation instructions, substrate/fixing design, exposure conditions, and competent review where safety or regulated performance is involved. Existing product pages retain quote and supply intent.

This domain does not create city/province/region articles. A location substitution is not a distinct editorial purpose. Related owned domains may cover the same entity from another useful perspective; same-domain cannibalization controls apply only within `phenolic.id`.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current Indonesian project requirements and authoritative primary sources before publication. Use applicable BSN/SNI, ministries/JDIH, fire/building/health requirements, manufacturer technical documents and SDS, accredited test reports, and project specifications; record edition/date and scope.
- Product identity must connect data sheet, batch/label, certificate/test specimen, supplied sheet, fabrication, and installed system. A certificate for a different construction, thickness, substrate, finish, or assembly is not proof.
- Never invent dimensions, tolerances, strength, water absorption, chemical resistance, fire class, antimicrobial reduction, emissions, service life, reuse count, price, warranty, or project results.
- Fire content requires competent fire review and distinguishes reaction-to-fire from fire resistance and product from assembly. Antibacterial/hygiene content requires exact organism, method, conditions, treated surface, result, cleaning durability, and reviewer competence.
- Exterior, structural, façade, formwork, vehicle-floor, fixing, and support claims require project-specific calculations or tested system tables used within scope.
- Machining/installation guidance starts with the current product manual and SDS; dust, chemical, fire, lifting, and tool hazards require current K3 controls and competent supervision.
- Original photos need permission, date, product/system identity, stage, and a statement of what the photo cannot prove. Case studies need real records; otherwise publish a method or checklist.
- Review Search Console by intent after publication. Merge or redraw pages when two same-domain URLs answer the same query job.
